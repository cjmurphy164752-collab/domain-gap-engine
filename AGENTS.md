# Contributor instructions

Keep gap observation separate from product invention. Do not add solutions,
purchase-intent guesses, numeric market scores or a forced count of findings.
Preserve provenance, remedies, counterevidence and explicitly unknown facts.
Metadata is discovery material, not substantive evidence.

Do not commit credentials, local setups, personal interests, imported evidence,
reports, SQLite state or n8n exports from a configured deployment. Generated
templates must have no credential or chat identifiers. Source access requires a
reviewed API or explicitly authorized local input; no general-purpose scraper.

Run focused tests, full tests and lint for changes. Preserve partial reports,
bounded resources, durable pending work and conservative delivery receipts.
Document limits instead of claiming validated market intelligence or security.
